# Changelog

What testers can do in each release. Versions follow the milestones: M2 is 0.2.0.

## 0.2.0: Outdoor logging (M2)

The first test release. Everything stays on the phone; accounts and sync come later (M11). Debug builds start with example sessions.

### What you can do

- **Find a crag or route.** Search by name. Æ, ø and å match their plain spellings. Nearby routes come first once you have logged a day outdoors.
- **Add a crag or route.** Add one that is missing, with grade, length, pitches and details. Pick the crag's location on a map.
- **Log a day outdoors.** Tap the log button, pick routes and how each climb went (onsight, flash, redpoint, attempt and more). Add your own grade, how the grade felt and tags. Sva checks the style and tries against what you climbed before.
- **Weather and season.** The weather fills in when there is signal. The season comes from the date and the crag.
- **See, edit and delete a session.** Open a session from the feed. Add a forgotten climb, remove one or change the texts. Later climbs on the same routes are recounted. Removing every climb deletes the session, after asking.
- **Your feed.** Sessions show as cards, newest first: where and when, the climbs with style and grade, details and totals.
- **Settings**, from the CV tab: theme (follow the phone, Midnight sun or Daylight), language (follow the phone, Norwegian or English), which grade systems routes and boulders show in, and who sees new sessions. About shows the version and licences.

### Known issues

- Gym sessions can't be logged yet (M3).
- Session cards have no photo or topo yet (M3, M5, M15).
- The grade pickers in the route form and the log sheet still use Norwegian and Font grades, whatever settings say. Grades are shown in your chosen system everywhere else.
- A theme or language other than the phone's can flash for a moment when the app starts.
- Nothing is shared with anyone yet, whatever the visibility setting says.
